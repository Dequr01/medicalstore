## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, clinical-grade digital pharmacy experience rooted in high trust, precision, and empathetic patient care. It merges sterile medical clarity with an approachable consumer wellness aesthetic.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Patients managing chronic prescriptions, health-conscious consumers seeking over-the-counter wellness goods, and caregivers ordering recurring medical supplies.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet confidence, uncompromising clinical safety, transparency, and calming efficiency during potentially stressful health moments.

### Design Movement: Clinical Modernism
The visual approach unites Swiss structural rigor with luminous, restorative accents:
- **Structural Purity:** Precise grid layouts, scannable vertical hierarchies, and crisp contrast ratios ensuring unambiguous legibility for dosages and medication instructions.
- **Warm Reassurance:** Tactile pill-shaped status tokens, soft clinical mint-tinted resting planes, and restrained ambient lighting that eliminate the cold dread often associated with institutional healthcare portals.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of pharmaceutical efficacy, sterile freshness, and reliable precision.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#0F766E` - Deep Clinical Teal):** Anchors primary brand touchpoints, critical call-to-actions, active navigation headers, and authoritative clinical verification stamps.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Vital Emerald / Mint):** Communicates restorative health, successful order/refill statuses, positive stock availability, active prescriptions, and wellness incentives.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Cerulean Rx Blue):** Applied selectively for telemetry details, informational dosage disclaimers, insurance coverage notices, and digital pharmacist chat touchpoints.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Provides high-contrast typographic rendering to ensure dosage instructions, pharmaceutical naming, and warning labels are effortlessly readable.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate Tint) creates a neutral, glare-free backdrop.
- **Surface Elevated:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Hospital White) for interactive cards, prescription panels, and clinical sheets.
- **Surface Subtle:** `#F0FDFA` (Ice Mint Mist) for verified badges, successful confirmation callouts, and highlighted dosage advisories.
- **Border Default:** `#E2E8F0` for micro-dividers and calm separation.
- **Border Focus / Verified:** `#99F6E4` for delicate teal ambient outlines.

## Typography

The dual-type strategy pairs the friendly, balanced geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**.

### Structural Application
- **Headings & Wayfinding (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Used for storefront categories, consultation section headers, and prescription milestones. Softened terminals reduce clinical anxiety while retaining high architectural authority.
- **Data, Instructions, & Dosage (Inter):** Deployed for drug facts, ingredient listings, dosage instructions, legal warnings, and UI controls. Inter's tall x-height and distinctive character apertures ensure mistakeproof readability under varied lighting and small screen environments.
- **Numbers & Tracking Identifiers:** Numbers in pharmaceutical lists and refill codes use tabular numbers to align decimal points and dosage milligrams cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly governed 8px vertical baseline grid is combined with a 12-column responsive fluid grid framework, delivering seamless transitions across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

### Grid & Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max container width `1440px`, with `margin: 3rem` outer buffers and `gutter: 1.5rem` between modules. Pharmacopeia catalogs and prescription management split into 8-column primary workflows and 4-column contextual summaries (e.g., cart, dosage schedule).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Secondary summary sidebars collapse into persistent bottom drawers or inline accordions.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Cart access and prescription status transitions become anchored to the bottom system safe area.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Component grouping enforces clear spatial proximity: label-to-input gap is locked to `space-xs` (4px), list-item-to-details gap to `space-sm` (8px), internal card padding to `space-lg` (24px), and cross-section boundaries to `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid model of **ambient shadows**, **micro-borders**, and **tonal surface stacking** to evoke sterile lucidity without flattening interactive affordances.

### Depth Architecture
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` solid fill. Provides a stable ground plane for resting components.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Product Tiles):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px micro-border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-diffused resting shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Medicine Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward translation (-2px), paired with an amplified teal-tinted ambient shadow: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.08)`. Border shifts to `#CCFBF1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Pharmacist Rails & Modals):** White `#FFFFFF` backdrop backed by deep floating containment: `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Dosage Warnings & Banners):** Flat, tinted solid plane `#F0FDFA` enclosed by a 1px border of `#99F6E4` with zero drop shadow to prioritize unadorned comprehension.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced curvature model (`roundedness: 2`, 8px base radius) paired with specialized pill geometry for clinical signifiers.

### Shape Geometry Directives
- **Panels, Cards, Modals:** Controlled `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius for standard structural components, scaling to `12px` (`0.75rem`) for prescription detail sheets and checkout containers.
- **Pill Badges & Medicine Indicators:** Full organic curvature (`9999px`) applied to prescription status markers (`Rx Required`, `In Stock`, `Refill Ready`), filter chips, dosage caps, and avatar containers.
- **Input Fields & Action Controls:** Uniform `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner rounding, instilling a solid, dependable feel during medical information entry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action/Refill):** Deep Clinical Teal (`#0F766E`) background, crisp white text, 8px corner radius. Internal padding: `12px 20px` (Desktop), `14px 24px` (Mobile). Hover state: darken to `#115E59` with subtle inward ring `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Active state: scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Add to Cart / View Details):** `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F766E`. Hover: background shifts to `#F0FDFA` with border color transition to `#0F766E`.
- **Emergency / Pharmacist Help:** Ghost button with `#0F766E` text and an integrated teal pulse icon dot.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Prescription Badge (Rx):** High-contrast pill. Deep Teal background (`#0F766E`) or Mint tint (`#ECFDF5`), bold uppercase text (`label-sm`), `4px 10px` padding, fully rounded (`9999px`).
- **Stock & Health Status:** Green tint (`#ECFDF5`) background, `#047857` text, prepended with a 6px circular green indicator light.
- **Filter Chips:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `6px 14px` padding. When active: filled `#0F766E`, white text, border `#0F766E`.

### Cards (Medication & Product)
- **Structure:** Clean vertical stack. `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `16px` inner padding.
- **Visual Zone:** Top image zone framed in `#F8FAFC` with a subtle inset border, isolating pill packaging or medication bottles against a neutral clean field.
- **Metadata Stack:** Category and Rx requirement chips anchored at the top, bold product name (`headline-sm`), milligram strength in `#64748B`, ending with a tabular price indicator and an accessible 1-tap refill button.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** 44px min touch height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px radius.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#0F766E` complemented by a 3px smooth outer halo of `rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.15)`.
- **Validation:** Dosage validation and insurance card numbers feature immediate inline confirmation ticks in `#10B981` or clear error states in `#DC2626` accompanied by clear iconographic flags.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20x20px square with a 4px corner radius. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Checked: solid `#0F766E` fill with a sharp white checkmark vector.
- **Radio Buttons:** 20x20px circle. Checked: `#FFFFFF` base with a 6px inner dot of `#0F766E` and an outer ring of `#0F766E`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Dosage Schedule Timeline:** Vertical rail connecting daily dosage nodes with alternating status pills (`Taken`, `Upcoming`, `Missed`) in `#10B981` and `#94A3B8`.
- **Prescription Upload Dropzone:** Drag-and-drop boundary with a dashed 1.5px border in `#99F6E4`, tinted `#F0FDFA` interior, and central high-visibility security shield icon highlighting HIPAA/regulatory data encryption.
