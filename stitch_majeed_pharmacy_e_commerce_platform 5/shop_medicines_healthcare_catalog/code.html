Important Interaction Notice: Statins & Grapefruit Metabolism

When ordering Atorvastatin or other HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors, avoid consuming grapefruit juice. Compounds in grapefruit inhibit the CYP3A4 enzyme in your gut, significantly increasing drug absorption and raising the risk of muscle toxicity or liver damage. Always check with our online dispensary team before introducing new herbal extracts.
